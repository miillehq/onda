03 · Settings

Settings people expect

Grouped the way people think about them, not how the engine is built. Every page opens with what most people need, and advanced options come last.

  1. 1Eight clear groups: General, Shortcuts, Microphone, Speech, AI polish, App styles, Privacy, Updates.
  2. 2A diagram instead of a paragraph: where your audio goes, and that it stays on your PC.
  3. 3Quality shown as a trade-off, speed against accuracy, with real download sizes from the model catalogue.
  4. 4Processor, storage and the full model list stay available, below the fold.
04 · Theme

Dark, designed on its own

Follows Windows by default. Not an inverted light mode: graphite surfaces stepped like the app icon’s tile, with the same contrast targets for text.

  1. ✓Light, dark and Windows high-contrast themes.
  2. ✓Respects Windows text size, animation and transparency settings.
